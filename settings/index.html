Your ESPN league

Link the league itself: import the draft order the moment it is drawn, and follow picks live on draft night. Needs the league id — the number after leagueId= in any ESPN league URL — and the league set to publicly viewable (League Settings → Basic Settings), because a browser cannot log in to ESPN on your behalf.

League private? Paste it instead

If your league manager keeps the league private, your own logged-in browser can still read it — the app just can't do it for you. While logged in to ESPN, enter your league id above, then open the link that appears here, select everything (Ctrl/Cmd+A), copy, and paste it below. It is league data only — no password, no cookies, and it never leaves this device.
